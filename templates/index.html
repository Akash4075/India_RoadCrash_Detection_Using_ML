Multi-Tier Scientific Architecture

Production Verified
Tier 1: National Risk Modeling Evaluates 6 situational parameters (Weather, Road Surface, Lighting, Traffic Control, Vehicle Category, and Speed Limits) with 84.83% verified accuracy.
Tier 2: Urban Hotspot Engine Synthesizes 207,617 Collision Avoidance System (CAS) fleet alerts across 50 BBMP municipal wards with 99.99% classification accuracy.
Tier 3: Karnataka GIS Ensemble Statewide geospatial model covering 39,051 police collision records (2016–2023) across 31 districts with 80.71% ensemble accuracy.
Sub-50ms Inference Latency All three inference pipelines are serialized into compact joblib model packages and run in-memory for immediate, reliable decision support.
