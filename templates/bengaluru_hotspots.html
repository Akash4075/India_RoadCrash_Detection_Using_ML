Data Provenance & Methodology Statement

Project Origin: Developed for MoveHack, the Global Mobility Hackathon conducted by NITI Aayog, Government of India. The project links IoT telemetry from vehicle onboard devices with historical BBMP ward accident and pothole classifications.

Limitations: Hotspot designations reflect historical sensor alert clusters (K-Means/GMM) from evaluated fleet vehicles in 2018. They do not constitute an active real-time traffic feed or a guarantee of future collision probability on specific road stretches.
